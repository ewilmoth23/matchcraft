# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in MatchCraft a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing experience, accountability, and careful protection of user privacy. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized attention, insulting or derogatory comments, publishing private information, or conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

MatchCraft-specific unacceptable behavior also includes sharing private résumés without informed permission, fabricating candidate credentials, encouraging discriminatory employment decisions, or weakening evidence and privacy protections without transparent review.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct and will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the repository's security or maintainer contact channel. Do not include a target's private résumé or contact data in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, with confidentiality respected as far as practical.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers may apply correction, warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban depending on impact, intent, and repeated behavior. Community safety and protection of private user data take priority.
