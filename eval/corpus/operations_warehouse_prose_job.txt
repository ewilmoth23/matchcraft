Warehouse Operations Supervisor at Kestrel Provisions Company

Kestrel Provisions moves refrigerated food to 140 independent grocers across three states, and the supervisor of our second shift is the person who keeps that promise every night. You will run a floor of 22 associates, set the pick and load sequence for roughly 40 outbound trailers, and hold the line on cold chain temperature compliance from receiving through dispatch.

We need at least four years of warehouse or distribution experience and at least two years supervising an hourly team, because coaching and scheduling are most of this job. A current forklift certification must be in hand before your first shift, and you must be comfortable working a full shift in a 34 degree environment.

Familiarity with a warehouse management system is expected. You should be able to pull throughput and accuracy reports from it without help from an analyst. Experience with OSHA recordkeeping and safety audits is preferred, as is exposure to continuous improvement methods such as Six Sigma. Bilingual English and Spanish is a plus on this shift.

You will report to the distribution manager and partner daily with transportation planning, receiving, and quality assurance.
