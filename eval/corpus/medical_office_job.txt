Medical Office Coordinator
Company: Thistlewood Family Health
Location: On-site - Boise, ID

Requirements
• 2+ years in a front office or patient facing coordination role.
• Experience scheduling appointments for multiple providers.
• Comfort handling insurance verification and patient billing questions.
• Familiarity with an electronic health record system is required.
• Working knowledge of HIPAA privacy obligations.

Preferred Qualifications
• Medical terminology coursework or certification is preferred.
• Experience with prior authorization workflows is preferred.
• Bilingual English and Spanish is a plus.

Responsibilities
• Check patients in and out and keep the daily schedule accurate.
• Verify coverage before visits and resolve claim rejections with billing.
• Protect patient information in every interaction.
