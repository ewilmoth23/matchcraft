Dispatch Operations Coordinator
Company: Foxglove Medical Supply
Location: On-site - Little Rock, AR

About Us
Foxglove Medical Supply delivers durable medical equipment to 300 clinics and 4,000 home patients across Arkansas.

Our Mission
We believe nobody should wait on the equipment that keeps them independent. We must all do our part to keep our neighbors healthy and supplied.

Requirements
• 3+ years of dispatch, routing, or field service coordination experience.
• Proven ability to manage a same-day exception queue under time pressure.
• Strong written and verbal communication with drivers, clinicians, and patients.
• Experience with a routing or fleet management platform is required.
• Accurate recordkeeping for controlled deliveries and proof of delivery.

Preferred Qualifications
• Experience in healthcare, pharmacy, or medical device logistics is preferred.
• Salesforce experience is preferred.
• Bilingual English and Spanish is a plus.

Responsibilities
• Build and adjust daily routes for 18 drivers across four service territories.
• Resolve delivery exceptions and communicate revised windows to clinics.
• Keep delivery documentation audit ready.

Benefits and Perks
• Medical, dental, and vision coverage starting on day one.
• A retirement plan with a 5% company match after 90 days.
• Bonus eligibility after your first full quarter.
• An annual learning stipend you can spend on anything from Excel classes to leadership coaching.
