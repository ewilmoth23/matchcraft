Commercial HVAC Service Technician
Company: Ironwood Mechanical Services
Location: On-site - Springfield, MO

• EPA Section 608 Universal certification is required.

• Boiler or hydronic heating experience is preferred.
• Commercial refrigeration certification is a plus.

Responsibilities
